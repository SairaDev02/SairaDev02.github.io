# SairaDev02 home page

The site presents the owner's public GitHub repositories as a music playlist: a CD player picture with one disc per repository, a track list, and a now-playing dock.

## Language

### Player

**Disc**:
One repository as it appears in the CD player picture.
_Avoid_: CD, record

**CD player**:
The picture of the player with its discs, together with the LCD faceplate beneath it.

**Dock**:
The fixed now-playing bar at the bottom of the page, with its Lyrics and Queue panels.
_Avoid_: music player, now-playing bar

**Cue**:
The Dock's current track, which its Play button plays.
_Avoid_: selection, up next (as a noun)

**Queue**:
The order the Dock steps through: Prev and Next follow it, and a Return moves the Cue to the next disc in it.
_Avoid_: up next, playlist

**Preview**:
A disc's details shown while it is pointed at or focused; it changes nothing else.
_Avoid_: hover (as a domain term)

**Play**:
A disc's timed stay in the CD player, begun by opening its repository in a new tab.
_Avoid_: navigate, listen

**Load**:
The lead-in of a play, in which the disc travels into the tray.
_Avoid_: insert

**Eject**:
Ending a play before its time is up.
_Avoid_: stop, pause

**Return**:
The disc going back from the tray when a play ends.

**Blocked tab**:
A tab the browser refused to open: its repository never plays, and the faceplate says so until the next Preview, drag or play.
_Avoid_: popup
